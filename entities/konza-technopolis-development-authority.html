OAG Kenya Findings · Tier A Profile

Konza Technopolis Development Authority

Register count: 6 Confirmed against source reports: 4

FY coverage of the findings below: FY2022/23 – FY2023/24

About this profile: Sourced from public OAG audit reports; not a RETACH assessment or rating. Each finding below is drawn directly from a published Auditor-General report, re-verified against the source PDF, and cited by financial year and section. This is not a comprehensive list of every finding in these reports — only findings classified as digital-risk findings under the S.I.N.S. Framework™ categories, including some the register missed. See the full methodology for how tiers, counts and this reconciliation are derived.
FY 2022/23 Internal Controls §1
The Konza National Data Centre's disaster-recovery site was not connected to a permanent, dedicated network link and so was not operational; it lacked some physical and environmental safeguards, and had not been tested. Data was backed up on the same servers as the primary data centre.
FY 2022/23 Internal Controls §2
Konza had not filled its Data Protection Act 2019 officer positions and had no data protection policy or data management strategy, despite holding data for more than 60 clients.
FY 2022/23 Internal Controls §3
Onboarding of data-centre customers and staff went no further than a form; no further background vetting was carried out.
FY 2023/24 Lawfulness §1
Of 23,000 virtual desktop infrastructure platforms and software purchased (valued at USD 3,992,974 and USD 8,951,194 respectively), 17,508 had been deployed and 5,492 remained idle in stores. Of those deployed, only 1,000 were in use and 16,508 were not connected.
